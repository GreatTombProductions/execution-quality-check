Method & sources

What this is

The amended SEC Rule 605 (compliance date 2026-08-01) requires market centers and large broker-dealers to publish, monthly, machine-readable order-execution reports in a standardized format: detailed reports as 55-field pipe-delimited files (Rule 605 NMS Plan, Exhibit A) and summary reports following the SEC’s Order Execution Summary (OES) CSV schema. The first reporting month is August 2026, due before the end of September 2026. This tool downloads the published files, verifies their hashes, and presents them so reporters can be compared — the comparability the standardized format exists to enable. It covers every reporter whose selected month file could be located and read (…); the Coverage page lists who is in and who is not.

Raw files come from the hosts each reporter’s Rule 605 page links: the exchanges’ own sites (Cboe’s CDN, IEX’s report archive, MIAX, MEMX, Nasdaq) and the shared Download Site host public.s3.com/rule605/, which publishes for most broker-dealers plus NYSE and 24X. A “published” time is the host’s Last-Modified header; a reporter may restate later (see Coverage).

Read the columns

  • Price improvement rate — share of executed orders’ quantity that was executed at a price better than the midpoint/quote, as published.
  • Weighted avg price improvement — share-weighted average improvement amount (as a fraction of midpoint).
  • Executed at quote or better — share executed at the quote or better.
  • Effective spread vs quoted spread — as a percentage of the midpoint. Smaller effective spread relative to the quoted spread means less of the quoted spread was paid.
  • EFQ — the effective-to-quoted-spread ratio (effective ÷ quoted; 100% means executions on average paid the full quoted spread).
  • Realized spread (15s / 1 min) — effective spread relative to the midpoint 15 seconds / 1 minute after execution; a market-impact measure.
  • Avg execution time — share-weighted average time from order receipt to execution, in the time units as reported (milliseconds in the OES report layout).

Executed shares throughout this tool means shares executed at the reporter plus shares executed at other venues (detailed-file fields 12 and 13). The Rule 605 plan defines price-improved shares and the spread averages over executions of covered orders generally, not only the reporter’s own executions: in these filings price-improved shares never exceed that total on any row, while for routing brokers they exceed their own executions alone by orders of magnitude. Weighted statistics use the same total.

    Caveats

    • All statistics are self-published by the venues and are not audited or verified by the SEC — this tool re-presents them, it does not bless them.
    • Reporters differ in market structure and computation methods; exchanges, wholesalers, broker-dealers and trading systems are not apples-to-apples (each reporter’s own filings say as much). Small reporters file few rows; a statistic from a handful of orders is not a ranking.
    • This is the first month of the standardized format. Early filings may be corrected or restated (MIAX Pearl already restated its August file once); the tool documents what was filed at build time.
    • Reporters’ covered orders overlap: a broker’s order that executes on an exchange can appear in both reports, so volumes must not be summed across reporters or read as market totals.
    • Per-symbol statistics are our share-weighted aggregates of the venues’ per-symbol rows (the venues publish no per-symbol aggregates) and exclude rows failing the sanity filters above.

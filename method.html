Method & sources

What this is

The amended SEC Rule 605 (compliance date 2026-08-01) requires market centers and large broker-dealers to publish, monthly, machine-readable order-execution reports in a standardized format: detailed reports as 55-field pipe-delimited files (Rule 605 NMS Plan, Exhibit A) and summary reports following the SEC’s Order Execution Summary (OES) CSV schema. The first reporting month is August 2026, due before the end of September 2026. This tool downloads the published files, verifies their hashes, and presents them so venues can be compared — the comparability the standardized format exists to enable.

Read the columns

  • Price improvement rate — share of executed orders’ quantity that was executed at a price better than the midpoint/quote, as published.
  • Weighted avg price improvement — share-weighted average improvement amount (as a fraction of midpoint).
  • Executed at quote or better — share executed at the quote or better.
  • Effective spread vs quoted spread — as a percentage of the midpoint. Smaller effective spread relative to the quoted spread means less of the quoted spread was paid.
  • EFQ — the effective-to-quoted-spread ratio (effective ÷ quoted; 100% means executions on average paid the full quoted spread).
  • Realized spread (15s / 1 min) — effective spread relative to the midpoint 15 seconds / 1 minute after execution; a market-impact measure.
  • Avg execution time — share-weighted average time from order receipt to execution, in the time units as reported by the venue (millisecond or finer increments).

    Caveats

    • All statistics are self-published by the venues and are not audited or verified by the SEC — this tool re-presents them, it does not bless them.
    • Venues differ in market structure and computation methods; averages across venue types are not apples-to-apples (each venue’s own filings say as much).
    • This is the first month of the standardized format. Publishers are still coming online and early filings may be corrected or restated; the tool documents what was filed at build time.
    • Per-symbol statistics are our share-weighted aggregates of the venues’ per-symbol rows (the venues publish no per-symbol aggregates) and exclude rows failing the sanity filters above.
    • Informational only — not investment advice.
